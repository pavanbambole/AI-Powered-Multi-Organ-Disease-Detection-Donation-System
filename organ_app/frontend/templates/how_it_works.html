Clinical AI Architecture

How MultiOrganAI Works

A transparent look at the multi-stage machine learning and natural language OCR pipeline that powers our diagnostics.

01

Data Ingestion & OCR Normalization

Users can submit clinical values manually through calibrated dynamic forms or upload diagnostic report images (PNG, JPG, PDF). Our preprocessing engine applies adaptive thresholding, contrast enhancement, and Optical Character Recognition (pytesseract) to identify laboratory markers (e.g., serum creatinine, ALT/AST, cholesterol) across diverse hospital formats.

02

Feature Scaling & Organ Model Inference

Normalized parameters pass into organ-specific supervised learning ensembles (Gradient Boosting and Random Forests). These models were trained with standard clinical cohorts to map multi-dimensional biomarker non-linearities into a calibrated risk score (0% to 100%) and confidence index.

03

Clinical Analyzer & Risk Stratification

The medical analyzer evaluates each parameter against established physiological reference intervals. Out-of-range biomarkers are classified as elevated, low, or critically aberrant. Tailored lifestyle interventions, dietary protocols (e.g., sodium restriction, glycemic management), and suggested diagnostic follow-ups are automatically synthesized.

04

Ethical Organ Matching Engine

For patients requiring transplantation support, our intelligent matching engine evaluates donor pools. Matching accounts for ABO/Rh blood compatibility, immunological parity, geographic transit distance (minimizing cold ischemia injury), and clinical urgency level (Critical ICU, High, Standard).
