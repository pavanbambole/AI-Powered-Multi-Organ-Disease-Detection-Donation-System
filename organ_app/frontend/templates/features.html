Renal Engine

Kidney Disease Diagnostic Engine

Evaluates glomerular filtration function, proteinuria grading, serum creatinine dynamics, and blood urea nitrogen. Accurately flags early stages of chronic kidney disease (CKD) before irreversible functional decline.

Key Markers: Serum Creatinine, Blood Urea, Urine Albumin, Hemoglobin, Specific Gravity.
Hepatic Engine

Hepatic Pathology & LFT Analyzer

Classifies hepatocellular damage vs. cholestatic biliary patterns. Evaluates transaminases (ALT, AST), alkaline phosphatase, and albumin synthesis margins to detect early fatty liver, hepatitis, or cirrhosis.

Key Markers: Total & Direct Bilirubin, ALT (SGPT), AST (SGOT), Alkaline Phosphatase, A/G Ratio.
Cardiovascular Health

Cardiovascular Risk Assessment

Integrates systemic vascular indicators, blood pressure dynamics, resting hemodynamics, and lipid balance to quantify global cardiovascular risk and inform timely clinical intervention.

Key Markers: Systolic / Diastolic BP, Heart Rate, Vascular Risk Index, Lipid Ratios.
